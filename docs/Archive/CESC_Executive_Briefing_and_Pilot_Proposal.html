3. Architecture & Zero-Risk Isolation Guarantees

The FlockML edge engine is designed with strict IT/OT network isolation. It operates purely as a passive telemetry consumer without any write-access to electrical breakers or SCADA actuation controls.

Zero-Interference Operational Guarantee:
  • Read-Only Telemetry Ingestion: Connects to read-only MQTT/Kafka smart meter mirrors or CSV data feeds.
  • Sandboxed Execution: Runs in lightweight WebGPU/WASM browser workers without requiring kernel drivers or OS modifications.
  • Sub-5ms Work-Stealing Failover: If any substation node loses power or connectivity, remaining cluster nodes automatically assume the workload without packet loss.
