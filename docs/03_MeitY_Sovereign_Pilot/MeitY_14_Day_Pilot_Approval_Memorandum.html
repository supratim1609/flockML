OFFICE MEMORANDUM
Subject:
Feasibility Study & 14-Day Pilot Authorization for 50-Node Sovereign WebGPU AI Compute Grid across NIC Delhi & Kolkata Facilities - reg.
1. The undersigned is directed to convey the technical concurrence of the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) to initiate a 14-day technical feasibility pilot for the FlockML Sovereign WebGPU AI Compute Grid Project (Project Lead: Shri Supratim Dhara). The pilot aims to assess distributed edge execution of 7B to 70B parameter Large Language Models (LLMs) over existing desktop workstation infrastructure.
2. The scope of the 14-day pilot deployment shall encompass a 50-node distributed cluster partitioned equally between National Informatics Centre (NIC) facilities as detailed below:
Facility Node Location Allocated Workstations Target Compute Capacity Primary Workload Scope
NIC CGO Complex, New Delhi 25 Desktop PCs 450 TFLOPS Aggregate BitNet 1.58-bit Quantized 7B/70B LLM Fine-Tuning
NIC Salt Lake Campus, Kolkata 25 Desktop PCs 450 TFLOPS Aggregate P2P Ring-AllReduce In-Browser Matrix Sharding
3. Zero Capital Outlay (₹0 Govt Expenditure): It is explicitly clarified that the execution of this 14-day feasibility pilot carries zero financial obligation or capital outlay on the part of MeitY / NIC. The orchestration layer utilizes existing idle desktop GPU/CPU cycles via WebGPU WGSL compute shaders, providing an estimated 68% operational cost advantage over commercial cloud GPU instances.
4. Data Sovereignty & Privacy Safeguards: In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, all prompt data and model weight updates shall strictly remain within local NIC network boundaries. Cryptographic integrity will be enforced via 0.1ms Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZK-SNARKs) and microsecond bitwise XOR WASM RAM shielding to ensure zero external data leakage.
5. Shri Ankit Tripathi, Program Officer (Emerging Technologies), NIC, is designated as the Nodal Officer to co-ordinate hardware access and technical monitoring alongside Shri Supratim Dhara (Lead Innovator, FlockML Initiative). Upon successful completion of the 14-day evaluation period, a detailed empirical performance report shall be submitted to the Director (AI Infrastructure Division).
6. This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
